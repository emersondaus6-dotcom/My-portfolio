Project Description

An automation project that utilizes different sensors and methor of actuation for system integration. The project s equipped with three systems: RFID, IR, and Sound detection.

  • RFID - A system where it scans the employees uniques to give access on the storage room
  • IR - The door is quipped with IR sensors that senses customers and automatically open the door
  • Sound sensor - The lights are controlled by clapping. Clap thrice to activate the lights and clap twice to turn it off
    • The project is controlloed by esp32 and arduino uno.
